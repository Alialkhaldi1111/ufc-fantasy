## Executive Summary

Junk It is a full-service junk removal company serving Ottawa, Ontario and surrounding communities. The company offers same-day removal of household junk, furniture, appliances, renovation debris, and complete estate and property cleanouts, with a commitment to diverting at least 50% of every load from landfill through donation and recycling.

The Ottawa junk removal market is established but fragmented: no single local operator dominates search results or reviews, and several competitors do not publish pricing. Junk It will win on three fronts: genuine same-day service, transparent published pricing, and a verifiable landfill-diversion story that resonates with Ottawa's environmentally conscious customer base.

Startup capital required is approximately $40,000 (financing a used truck) with break-even at roughly 36 jobs per month, expected by month 3–4. Year-one revenue is projected at $225,000–$230,000 with owner pre-tax income of $65,000–$80,000.

## 1. Business Overview

### 1.1 Name

Working name: **Junk It**. The name is short, memorable, and action-oriented, and reads well on a truck wrap and in search results.

**Name-clearance caution:** "Junk It" carries meaningfully higher conflict risk than a distinctive coined name. An established junk removal operator already uses the "Junk It!" brand in the Greater Toronto Area (junkit.ca), and generic "junk" names are crowded across Ontario. Before incorporating, run a NUANS search, check the Canadian trademark register (CIPO), and confirm the .ca/.com domain and Google Business Profile situation. If the NUANS or trademark search shows a conflict, be prepared to fall back to a distinguishable variant (e.g., "Junk It Ottawa") or an alternate name. Also note that "Bytown Haul" already operates in Ottawa (avoid Bytown-based names) and "ClearPath Ottawa Junk" exists (avoid ClearOut/ClearPath confusion). The name must be confirmed via NUANS search at incorporation.

### 1.2 Mission

"Same-day, honest-price junk removal for Ottawa homes and businesses — with a commitment to diverting at least 50% of every load from landfill through donation and recycling."

### 1.3 Target Market Segments (year-one priority order)

- **Residential cleanouts** — basements, garages, moving preparation. Highest margins; easiest to win through Google.
- **Estate cleanouts** — high-value jobs ($800–$3,000+) sourced through realtors, estate lawyers, and retirement homes. Ottawa's aging population makes this a growing segment.
- **Commercial** — office cleanouts, retail closures, property-management turnovers. Recurring revenue potential.
- **Light construction/renovation debris** — drywall, flooring, fixtures from contractors. Steady volume but price-competitive; commercial and C&D waste cannot go to the city landfill (see Section 6).

## 3. Legal Setup (Ontario)

| Step | Detail | Approx. cost |
|---|---|---|
| Incorporation | Ontario incorporation recommended for liability protection; via Ontario Business Registry | ~$300 + ~$30 NUANS |
| HST registration | Mandatory over $30,000/yr revenue; register day one to claim input tax credits | Free (CRA) |
| Commercial General Liability | $2M minimum; commercial clients demand proof | $1,500–$3,000/yr |
| Commercial auto insurance | Truck insured for business use and cargo | $3,000–$6,000/yr |
| WSIB | Mandatory on first hire; owner optional coverage; ~3–5% of payroll | % of payroll |
| CVOR certificate | Required for commercial trucks with RGW over 4,500 kg (most 1-ton + trailer combos) | ~$300 + compliance |
| EASR waste registration | Ontario waste-hauling systems generally register via EASR; confirm with MECP before first commercial job | $100–$1,000 |
| Municipal check | Ottawa has no junk-removal licence per se; confirm via 3-1-1 | Minimal |

**Excluded materials (never accept):** asbestos, liquid waste, hazardous/chemical waste, propane tanks in loads. Screen at booking.

## 5. Equipment & Startup Costs

### 5.1 Vehicle

Recommended: a used 2019–2021 one-ton (F-350 / RAM 3500) with a 12–14 cu-yd dump box or dump trailer, approximately $35,000–$50,000 all-in, financed at $700–$900/month. Alternative low-cost entry: 3/4-ton pickup plus 14-ft dump trailer ($15,000–$25,000 if a truck is already owned).

### 5.2 Tools & Equipment (~$3,500)

Appliance dolly, 4-wheel dollies, furniture straps and blankets, demolition tools (sledgehammer, pry bars, reciprocating saw for hot tub/piano breakdowns), tarps, shovels, PPE. Steel-toed boots and hi-vis vests are mandatory at Trail Road tipping areas.

### 5.3 Dump Fees — Trail Waste Facility (4475 Trail Rd)

| Material | Rate | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Residential garbage | $157/tonne | $43.20 (<250 kg) |
| Mixed garbage & recyclables (unsorted) | $314/tonne | $78.50 |
| Fill (concrete, masonry, asphalt) | $144/tonne | $36.00 |
| Brush / yard waste | $129/tonne | $12.90 |
| Scrap metal, e-waste, tires (max 10) | FREE | — |

**Critical constraint:** Trail Road does not accept commercial garbage (IC&I ban in effect). Commercial, construction, and office-job waste must go to private transfer stations: GFL Ottawa Waste Transfer, Waste Connections Ottawa Transfer Station, or Tomlinson Waste Recovery Centre (Carp). Private tipping typically runs $130–$200/tonne; open charge accounts at all three. The $314/tonne mixed-load penalty means sorting pays: pulling metal, e-waste, and cardboard from loads roughly halves disposal cost.

## 6. Operations

### 6.1 Disposal, Recycling & Donation Network

- **Trail Road** — residential garbage; free metal, e-waste, tires
- **GFL, Waste Connections, Tomlinson (Carp)** — commercial and C&D loads (charge accounts)
- **Scrap yards (e.g., Bakermet/AIM)** — they pay for appliances, BBQs, metal furniture
- **Donations** — Habitat for Humanity ReStore (2 Ottawa locations), Salvation Army Thrift, Value Village, Matthew House furniture bank, St. Vincent de Paul
- **Mattresses** — recycling programs ~$25–$40/unit, cheaper than the mixed-load rate

### 6.2 Staffing

- **Months 1–3:** owner + part-time helper on call ($20–$22/hr) for two-person items; WSIB from first hire
- **Months 4–12:** one full-time helper (~$45,000/yr fully loaded)
- **Hiring criteria:** clean G licence, repeated 50-lb lifts, customer-friendly — the crew is the brand

### 6.3 Scheduling & Dispatch

- **Software:** Jobber or Workiz ($100–$200/mo) — booking, quotes, invoicing, payment, routing, review requests
- 2-hour arrival windows; 3–5 jobs/day per truck; end each day at the transfer station to start empty
- Photo quotes by text for small jobs; free on-site quotes for cleanouts (80%+ conversion)
- Payment by card or e-transfer on completion; never invoice residential customers

## 7. Marketing Plan

### 7.1 Local SEO & Google (~70% of leads)

- **Google Business Profile** — highest-ROI asset. Post weekly, photograph every job, automate review requests. Target 50+ reviews in year one for map-pack top three.
- **Google Local Services Ads** — pay-per-lead ($25–$50/lead), Google Guaranteed badge, on from day one
- **Website** — published price table; one page per service and per neighbourhood (Kanata, Orleans, Barrhaven, Nepean, downtown)
- **Google Ads search** — start at $1,000/mo on high-intent terms

### 7.2 Partnerships (highest-value channel)

- Realtors (pre-listing cleanouts), property managers (turnover cleanouts), estate lawyers and executors, renovation contractors, storage facilities (abandoned units)

### 7.3 Social & Community

- Before/after content on Facebook/Instagram; Ottawa neighbourhood Facebook groups; Kijiji listing; phone-number-forward truck wrap as a rolling billboard

### 7.4 Seasonal Promotions

- **April–May:** Spring Cleanout bundle · **June–July:** moving-season realtor push · **September:** student turnover · **November:** pre-snow clearout · **January:** 15% New-Year declutter to fill the slowest month

## 9. Growth Roadmap

- **Phase 1 — Prove it (months 1–12):** one truck, owner-operated. Goals: 50+ Google reviews, 3 jobs/day average, 5 recurring commercial accounts, systems documented in Jobber.
- **Phase 2 — Off the truck (months 12–20):** hire crew lead + helper; owner shifts to sales and partnerships. Add Truck 2 (~$50,000) when Truck 1 books 2+ days out. Two trucks ≈ $450,000–$500,000 revenue capacity.
- **Phase 3 — Multi-crew (years 2–3):** three trucks, 6–7 field staff, part-time dispatcher. Add dumpster-bin rentals (~$30,000/bin-set, high margin, smooths winter). Pursue standing PM and insurance-restoration contracts.
- **Phase 4 — Regional (year 3+):** Gatineau (separate Quebec registration required), Kemptville, Arnprior corridors. Consider resale of high-value cleanout finds (10–15% of revenue at mature junk firms).
