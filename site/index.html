FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. For curbside or garage pickups, we can confirm the price by text with photos, collect payment by e-transfer or card, and send you a photo of the cleared space when we're done.

Can you really come today?

Usually, yes. Same-day slots depend on the day's schedule. Book in the morning and we can typically get to you the same afternoon. A $50 same-day surcharge applies only when we're fully booked, and we tell you before booking.

How do you price a job?

By how much space your stuff takes in the truck, using the published table above. You see the truck, you see the space it takes, and you approve the firm price before we load a single item.

What won't you take?

Asbestos, liquid waste, hazardous chemicals and propane tanks. Everything else, from a single mattress to a whole house, is fair game.

How do I pay?

Card or e-transfer when the job is done. No deposits, no invoices, no surprises.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry commercial general liability insurance, so your home, your building and our crew are covered on every job.
